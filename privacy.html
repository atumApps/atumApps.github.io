Privacy Policy

In Tiny Evolution, Google Mobile Ads may use the advertising ID of your device to serve targeted ads. Tiny Evolution uses Google analytics to monitor and improve the app experience. Further details of how your personal data is handled through Google Analytics can be found here.

Inkhorn sends us anonymous gameplay stats to help balance the game, which can be turned off in the app's settings, and needs internet access to do so. It is also available on Android, where optional achievements and the purchase of the app are handled by Google. Full details are in the Inkhorn privacy policy.

All other apps collect no personal data at all and do not transmit any logging data back to the developers (us).

All our apps require no device permissions beyond internet access. In order to access optional Game Center achievements, users must login with their Game Center account, which is handled by Apple.

In order to purchase optional additional content, users must complete a payment transaction, which is handled by Apple.
